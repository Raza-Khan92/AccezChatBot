## How do I sign up as a service provider on Accez?
You create your account on the Accez service provider portal at provider.accez.cloud/sp/signup. The account type is fixed to Service Provider on that page, so you do not need to choose anything extra. Once your account exists, you build your listing with your photos, category, location, opening hours and priced services. Accez describes the setup as: sign up, add services, set weekly hours, add team members, then connect Stripe to take payments.
<!-- src: SP-001, WEB-068, DOC-224 -->

## What information does the service provider sign-up form ask for?
The sign-up form on the Accez service provider portal asks for your first name, last name, email, country code and phone number, a password, and the password again to confirm it. The phone number must be 6 to 14 digits once spaces and dashes are removed. You also need to tick a box agreeing to receive SMS messages and a box accepting the terms before you can submit the form.
<!-- src: SP-002, SP-004, SP-005 -->

## Do I have to complete a security check when I sign up?
Yes. The sign-up form on the Accez service provider portal includes a Google reCAPTCHA. The Create Account button stays disabled until you have completed it. After you submit the form you also need to verify your contact details, which is explained in the next step of the sign-up process.
<!-- src: SP-006 -->

## How do I verify my email and phone number after signing up?
After you sign up on the Accez service provider portal, you verify your email with a 6-digit code and your phone number with a 4-digit code. When both are verified, the portal takes you to business setup, where you choose a plan and enter your business details. Keep your email and phone handy while you sign up so you can enter both codes.
<!-- src: SP-007, SP-009 -->

## Is a service provider account separate from a property manager account?
Yes. On Accez, Service Provider is its own account type and is completely separate from a property manager account. Provider pricing is also separate from the Accez property management plans. If you run a service business and want to be listed on the Accez marketplace, you sign up as a service provider rather than as a property manager.
<!-- src: WEB-066, WEB-082 -->

## What kinds of businesses can list on the Accez marketplace?
Any kind of service business can become a provider on the Accez marketplace, not only salons and barbers. Examples include salons and barbers, spas and wellness studios, fitness coaches, photographers, home services and a pet clinic. Accez says any independent business can join, not only property-related trades. If your business type is not in the setup list, you can choose Other.
<!-- src: D-02, WEB-066, SP-013 -->

## Who are the people involved in the service provider side of Accez?
The service provider side of Accez has three roles: the business owner (the vendor admin), the staff members the owner adds, and the customers who book. There are three matching surfaces. Owners use the Accez service provider portal for services, team, calendar and earnings. Customers use the Accez marketplace to discover and book. Staff use a simpler dashboard for their own schedule and bookings.
<!-- src: DOC-204, DOC-205 -->

## What happens after I verify my account?
After verifying your email and phone, the Accez service provider portal sends you to business setup. Setup is a chat-style wizard with two phases: first you choose a plan, then you enter your business details. Once your profile is saved, you are taken to the Billing page, where you can connect Stripe to take payments.
<!-- src: SP-009, SP-011, SP-016 -->

## What does the business setup wizard ask me?
In the Accez service provider portal, the setup wizard asks for your business name (at least 2 characters), business type, industry, business phone, an optional website, your monthly marketing budget and your business address. The marketing budget choices are shown in SAR: under 1,000, 1,000 to 5,000, 5,000 to 20,000, and 20,000 or more.
<!-- src: SP-012, SP-015 -->

## Which business types and industries can I pick during setup?
In the Accez service provider portal setup, the business type choices are Salon, Spa, Barber shop, Nail bar, Wellness or Massage, Fitness or Personal trainer, and Other. The industry choices are Beauty and Grooming, Health and Wellness, Fitness, Home Services, Professional Services and Other. If nothing fits your business exactly, such as a pet clinic, choose Other.
<!-- src: SP-013, SP-014, D-02 -->

## Does the setup wizard save my progress if I refresh the page?
No. The business setup wizard in the Accez service provider portal does not cache your progress, so refreshing the page restarts it. It is best to keep the page open and finish the wizard in one go, with your business name, phone, address and other details ready before you start.
<!-- src: DOC-215 -->

## When does my business show up on the marketplace?
A business appears on the public Accez marketplace after its profile has been saved in the Accez service provider portal. Your listing shows your photos, an about section, your location and directions, your opening hours for each day and your service list. Accez says you can have your listing live in minutes once your details are in.
<!-- src: SP-017, WEB-070, WEB-069 -->

## How do I log in to the provider portal later?
You can log in to the Accez service provider portal at provider.accez.cloud/sp/login. Staff members and business owners use the same login page. After signing in, owners see the full provider menu and staff members are taken to their own trimmed-down dashboard.
<!-- src: SP-181, WEB-012, DOC-238 -->

## What plans can a service provider choose from?
Accez has three plans for service providers: Free, Starter and Growth. Every plan includes a listing on the Accez marketplace. You can see the plan list before you log in, and you choose your plan during business setup in the Accez service provider portal. For anything beyond plan names, prices and the Free-plan fee, please check the plan page or ask the Accez team.
<!-- src: D-03, WEB-083, SP-021, DOC-207 -->

## What does the Free provider plan cost?
The Free plan on the Accez marketplace has no monthly subscription. Instead, a standard platform fee applies to the bookings a provider receives on the Free plan. It is a way to get listed and start taking bookings without a monthly cost, and it includes a public marketplace listing, the provider dashboard, and ratings and reviews.
<!-- src: D-02, WEB-079, DOC-208 -->

## How much do the Starter and Growth plans cost per month?
On the Accez website, the Starter plan is SAR 75 per month and the Growth plan is SAR 184 per month. Both are monthly subscriptions for service providers on the Accez marketplace. Growth is marked as the most popular plan. Provider pricing is separate from Accez property management plans.
<!-- src: WEB-080, WEB-081, WEB-082 -->

## How do I start on the Free plan, and how do I pay for a paid plan?
Choosing the Free plan in the Accez service provider portal activates it straight away, with no card needed. A paid plan is bought through Stripe Checkout, which is hosted by Stripe. After you pay, your plan is activated. If it does not show as active, please contact the Accez team.
<!-- src: SP-039, SP-040, DOC-216 -->

## What if I close the page after paying on Stripe Checkout?
If you leave after Stripe Checkout before the success page appears, the next time you log in to the Accez service provider portal you are sent back to the plan picker until your subscription is active. A late confirmation from Stripe will not activate the plan twice. If payment was not completed, the portal shows a message asking you to try again.
<!-- src: DOC-217, DOC-216 -->

## How do I set up the services I offer?
In the Accez service provider portal you organise what you offer into categories, and each category contains services. Open the Services page to create a category, then add services inside it. Each service has its own details and price, and you decide which ones customers can see on your public listing on the Accez marketplace.
<!-- src: SP-060, DOC-227, WEB-071 -->

## What can I set on a service category?
A category in the Accez service provider portal has a name, a description, an optional image and a toggle that switches it between Visible to customers and Hidden. Use Hidden when you want to prepare a category without showing it on your marketplace listing yet.
<!-- src: SP-061 -->

## What can I set on an individual service?
Each service in the Accez service provider portal has a name, description, image, currency, a pricing model, a visibility toggle, and the team members who provide it. Every service has its own duration and price. You can show or hide a service from customers at any time.
<!-- src: SP-062, DOC-227, WEB-071 -->

## What is the difference between fixed and timely pricing?
In the Accez service provider portal, a fixed-price service has one price and one duration in minutes. A timely service has several tiers, each with its own duration and price, for example a short and a long version of the same service. The price must be zero or more, and a timely service needs at least one tier.
<!-- src: SP-063, SP-064 -->

## Which currency are my service prices in?
Each service has a currency. By default it uses your business default currency, and if none is set it falls back to SAR. The default currency is a required field in Business Settings in the Accez service provider portal, so you can set it once and keep your services consistent.
<!-- src: SP-065, SP-178 -->

## Do I have to set a duration on every service?
Yes. Customers on the Accez marketplace cannot complete a booking if the selected services add up to zero minutes. Make sure every service in the Accez service provider portal has a duration, either one fixed duration or the durations on each timely pricing tier.
<!-- src: SP-066, SP-063 -->

## What happens if I delete a service category?
In the Accez service provider portal, deleting a category asks you to confirm first, and it also deletes all the services inside it. If you only want to stop customers seeing a category for a while, use the Hidden toggle instead of deleting it.
<!-- src: SP-067, SP-061 -->

## Can I add staff members to my provider account?
Yes. In the Accez service provider portal, you add team members from the Team page. You give a first name and email, and can add a last name, phone, bio and specialty tags. There is also an Available for booking toggle. Plan details for team size are shown on the plan page, so check there or ask the Accez team.
<!-- src: SP-069, DOC-226 -->

## What happens when I add a team member?
When you add a team member in the Accez service provider portal, Accez creates a login for their email address and emails them their sign-in details. If that email already belongs to an Accez account, no new account is made; they get an email saying they were added and can sign in with their existing password.
<!-- src: SP-070, SP-071 -->

## Can I stop a team member from taking bookings?
Yes. You can mark a team member as inactive in the Accez service provider portal, shown as Off-duty or hidden. They are then not bookable on the Accez marketplace. You can turn their availability back on later from the Team page.
<!-- src: SP-073 -->

## How do I decide which team members perform which services?
In the Accez service provider portal you can link each service to the team members who perform it. A team member with no linked services counts as a generalist who can take any service. Customers only get matched with people who can actually perform every service they selected.
<!-- src: SP-074, SP-076 -->

## Can customers choose which team member they book?
Yes. When booking on the Accez marketplace, customers can pick a specific team member or choose Any available. With Any available, the system assigns the first active team member who covers every service and is free in that time window. Solo providers with no team do not show a staff picker.
<!-- src: SP-075, SP-076, DOC-269 -->

## What can my staff see and do in the portal?
Staff log in to the same Accez portal login page and see a trimmed dashboard with their own schedule, calendar, availability and profile pages. They can start a booking, mark it complete, or cancel it with a reason. They cannot reassign work or see colleagues' bookings or pricing.
<!-- src: SP-077, SP-078, DOC-238, DOC-239 -->

## Can team members add time off?
Yes. In the Accez service provider portal, a team member can add time off with a date range and an optional reason, and cancel it later. They can also see the business hours, which are read-only for them. Time off removes that person from the slots customers can book on those dates.
<!-- src: SP-080, SP-081, DOC-237 -->

## Can I move a booking to a different team member?
Yes. The business owner can open a booking from the Calendar in the Accez service provider portal and reassign it to another team member, or set it to Any available. The new person must cover all the services on the booking and be free at that time. Reassigning to an inactive team member is blocked.
<!-- src: SP-083, DOC-235 -->

## Can I use Accez if I work alone?
Yes. If you have not entered any staff, the Accez service provider portal treats your business as a one-person business that can take one booking at a time. Customers on the Accez marketplace will not see a staff picker, and the same hours and availability rules apply.
<!-- src: SP-089, DOC-269 -->

## How do I set my business hours?
In the Accez service provider portal you set weekly business hours on the Business Hours page. For each day you choose open or closed and can add one or more time slots, such as a morning and an afternoon block. Opening a closed day starts with a default 09:00 to 18:00 slot, and a business with every day closed shows as fully closed.
<!-- src: SP-085, DOC-228 -->

## What timezone do my hours and bookings use?
You choose a business timezone in the Policies page of the Accez service provider portal, and it defaults to Asia/Riyadh. Customers see and book slots in that timezone, so a customer in another country sees your slot in your local time. Changing the timezone reinterprets your existing hours, so check them afterwards.
<!-- src: SP-086, DOC-273 -->

## Can customers book outside my opening hours?
No. A scheduled booking outside your hours, or on a day you have marked closed, is rejected on the Accez marketplace. Customers can only book inside the hours you set in the Accez service provider portal.
<!-- src: SP-087, WEB-071 -->

## How does Accez decide which time slots customers can book?
On the Accez marketplace, slots are offered on a 30-minute grid by default. A slot is available while at least one eligible team member is free for the whole duration of the service, so three stylists can take three customers at the same time. A booking is refused if the time is already full or the chosen team member is already booked.
<!-- src: SP-088, SP-090 -->

## What does the Calendar page show?
The Calendar in the Accez service provider portal has Week and Day views. Bookings are colour-coded and each card shows the assigned team member. Click any booking to open its details and take action on it.
<!-- src: SP-092 -->

## What can I do with a booking from the Calendar?
Clicking a booking on the Calendar in the Accez service provider portal opens a drawer. From it you can confirm or reject the booking, start it, complete it, and reassign it to a different team member.
<!-- src: SP-093, SP-083 -->

## How do I manage bookings on the Bookings page?
The Bookings page in the Accez service provider portal lists your bookings with search and status counts. Each booking has a detail panel showing the services, the customer's note, deposit and balance, and payment status. You can confirm or reject a booking, start it, complete it, or mark it as a no-show.
<!-- src: SP-097, SP-100 -->

## Can I add private notes to a booking?
Yes. When you change a booking's status in the Accez service provider portal, you can add a private note of up to 2,000 characters. The note is for your own records, so you can keep track of anything worth remembering about that booking or customer.
<!-- src: SP-105 -->

## Can customers reschedule a booking?
Yes. A customer on the Accez marketplace can reschedule an upcoming booking from their account. Only the time moves, and the payment is not run again. The new slot goes through the same checks for your hours and availability as any new booking.
<!-- src: SP-102 -->

## Can customers cancel a booking?
A customer on the Accez marketplace can cancel an upcoming booking from their account. A completed booking cannot be cancelled. For questions about money on a cancelled booking, please contact the Accez team, because this depends on the booking.
<!-- src: SP-103, D-08 -->

## What is the waitlist?
Customers on the Accez marketplace can join a waitlist for a day that is fully booked. In the Accez service provider portal you see waiting and notified entries on the Waitlist page and can remove them. Waiting customers are emailed when a slot frees up, including when a booking is cancelled, rejected or marked no-show.
<!-- src: SP-171, SP-106 -->

## How does a customer book with me?
On the Accez marketplace, a customer picks the services, then a date and time, then reviews and confirms. Along the way they can choose a team member, use a gift card, use loyalty points, add a tip, and choose to make the booking recurring. Each booking reaches your Bookings page in the Accez service provider portal.
<!-- src: SP-155, DOC-266 -->

## Can customers make a booking repeat?
Yes. A customer on the Accez marketplace can make a booking recurring: weekly, every two weeks, or monthly. A nightly job then creates the next booking for each active recurring subscription automatically. This means repeat customers do not have to rebook each visit by hand.
<!-- src: SP-172, SP-173, DOC-254 -->

## What does the Customers page show?
The Customers page in the Accez service provider portal works as your client book. For each customer it shows visits, lifetime spend, no-shows, last visit, private notes and tags. You can filter by groups such as new, upcoming, top, lapsed and no-show customers.
<!-- src: SP-163 -->

## Can I email my customers?
Yes. From the Customers page in the Accez service provider portal you can email selected customers or a whole segment. Customers who have opted out of marketing are marked, so you can see who not to contact.
<!-- src: SP-164 -->

## What are Deals?
Deals let you send an offer to your customers from the Accez service provider portal. You write the offer, choose which customer segment receives it, and either send it now or schedule it. The offer is emailed to the chosen customers, and scheduled deals are sent automatically when they are due.
<!-- src: SP-165 -->

## Which payment providers does Accez use?
Payments on Accez run through Stripe and Tap Payments. As a provider you connect your bank through Stripe from the Billing page of the Accez service provider portal. If you have questions about which provider applies to your account or your customers, the Accez team can confirm.
<!-- src: D-10, SP-113 -->

## How can customers pay for a booking?
Customers on the Accez marketplace pay online through Stripe or Tap Payments. For how payment works on your specific plan, please ask the Accez team.
<!-- src: SP-110, D-10, D-22 -->

## Do I need to set up Stripe before I can take card bookings?
Yes. A card booking on the Accez marketplace is refused unless you have finished Stripe setup and card charges are enabled on your account. If setup is incomplete, customers see a message that the provider is not set up to accept online payments yet. You complete setup from the Billing page in the Accez service provider portal.
<!-- src: SP-112, SP-113 -->

## How do I connect my bank account through Stripe?
Open the Billing page in the Accez service provider portal and use the Bank setup tab. You complete a Stripe-hosted onboarding, and the page shows your progress: profile submitted, card payments enabled and payouts enabled. It also lists any information Stripe still needs from you. The Integrations page shows whether Stripe is connected.
<!-- src: SP-113, DOC-248, SP-143 -->

## How do I request a payout?
In the Payouts tab of the Accez service provider portal Billing page you see your available balance, in-transit balance and total withdrawn. You request a payout yourself, up to your available balance, because automatic payouts are turned off. Payouts only work once Stripe verification is complete and payouts are enabled. Pending payouts can be cancelled from your payout history.
<!-- src: SP-115, SP-116, SP-118, DOC-249 -->

## Where can I see my earnings?
The Transactions tab on the Billing page of the Accez service provider portal lists paid bookings with the gross amount, the fee and your earnings. You can also export an earnings summary for the last 6 months as a CSV file.
<!-- src: SP-120 -->

## Do tips go to me?
Customers on the Accez marketplace can add a tip, either a preset percentage or a custom amount, capped at the booking subtotal. Tips are not subject to platform fees and pass to you in full. The platform fee is calculated on the booking subtotal only, not on the tip.
<!-- src: SP-131, WEB-124, DOC-251 -->

## Can I ask customers to pay a deposit?
Yes. In the Policies page of the Accez service provider portal you can set an upfront deposit percentage from 0 to 100. At 0, customers pay in full. When a deposit is set, customers choose between paying in full or paying the deposit now, and you collect the balance after the service.
<!-- src: SP-123, SP-124, DOC-246 -->

## How do gift cards work for providers?
In the Gift Cards page of the Accez service provider portal you create gift card options with a label, amount, optional validity in months, description and availability. You can also see the cards that have been issued, with their balance and status.
<!-- src: SP-167 -->

## How do customers buy and use gift cards?
On the Accez marketplace, a customer buys a gift card for a recipient's email address. The recipient can redeem it at checkout as store credit, and the card's value reduces the amount charged to their card.
<!-- src: SP-168 -->

## Can I run a loyalty programme?
Yes. On the Loyalty page of the Accez service provider portal you can run a points programme with an on and off switch. You set how many points customers earn per currency unit, how many points are needed for a one-unit discount, and a minimum points threshold before they can redeem.
<!-- src: SP-169 -->

## How do customers earn and use loyalty points?
Customers on the Accez marketplace earn points on paid bookings with providers who run a loyalty programme. They can redeem their points at checkout for a discount, and the discount is capped by the value of the order.
<!-- src: SP-170 -->

## Who can leave a review?
A customer on the Accez marketplace can review a booking only after it is completed. They give a 1 to 5 rating for your business, can rate the team member separately, and can add a comment of up to 1,000 characters and up to 5 photos. Only one review is allowed per booking.
<!-- src: SP-145, SP-147, DOC-260 -->

## Can customers change their review?
Yes. A customer can edit their review within 7 days of leaving it. After that, the review stays as it is. Each booking can only have one review, so the edit replaces the original rather than adding a second one. This applies to every provider on the Accez marketplace.
<!-- src: SP-146, DOC-260 -->

## Where do reviews show up?
Every completed booking can earn a rating and review, shown on your listing on the Accez marketplace. Reviews are published as submitted. In the Accez service provider portal, the Reviews page shows your rating figures and a feed you can filter by stars, team member or unanswered reviews.
<!-- src: WEB-075, SP-148 -->

## Can I reply to a review?
Yes. On the Reviews page of the Accez service provider portal you can post a public reply to a review, edit it, or remove it. Each review can have one reply from you. Replies are public, so customers browsing your listing on the Accez marketplace can read them next to the review.
<!-- src: SP-148, DOC-261 -->

## Can I delete or report a review I disagree with?
You cannot delete a review just because you disagree with it. If a review is abusive, you can flag it in the Accez service provider portal. A flagged review is hidden from the public list and left out of your average until Accez support has handled it, and you cannot unflag it yourself.
<!-- src: WEB-075, SP-149, DOC-261, DOC-284 -->

## What emails do I and my customers receive about bookings?
In the Accez service provider portal, both you and the customer receive an email when a booking is created. The customer also receives an email whenever the booking status changes, for example when it is confirmed, started, completed, cancelled, rejected or marked no-show.
<!-- src: SP-099 -->

## Does Accez send reminders to my customers?
Customers receive reminders for confirmed bookings. You can switch customer reminders off from the Policies page in the Accez service provider portal if you prefer not to send them. Reminders only go out for confirmed bookings and the switch sits on the Policies page.
<!-- src: SP-159, SP-160 -->

## What does my public listing on the marketplace include?
Your public listing on the Accez marketplace shows your photos, an about section, your location with directions, your opening hours for every day of the week and your full service list. Customers can also see your reviews and a Book now button. Customers can only book inside the hours you set.
<!-- src: WEB-070, DOC-265, WEB-071 -->

## How do customers find providers on the Accez marketplace?
Customers browse by category, such as Beauty, Spa and Wellness, Fitness, Photography and Home services. They can also search by name and filter on a map. Search matches on business name, business type and industry, so a clear business name and the right type in the Accez service provider portal help customers find you.
<!-- src: SP-152, WEB-072 -->

## Can customers save or share my listing?
Yes. Signed-in customers on the Accez marketplace can save a provider as a favourite with a heart button, and anyone can share a provider page using the device share sheet or by copying the link. A Get directions button opens Google Maps with your address.
<!-- src: DOC-275, DOC-286, DOC-287 -->

## What does Accez do and not do as the marketplace?
Accez lists you and takes the booking. It does not perform the service, set your prices, or employ you or your staff. The Accez service provider portal lets you set up your business profile, publish services with durations and prices, manage your calendar and availability, add staff and assign bookings, and see your bookings and billing.
<!-- src: WEB-077 -->

## What is in the provider portal menu?
The menu in the Accez service provider portal includes Dashboard, Bookings, Calendar, Waitlist, Services, Gift Cards, Deals, Customers, Loyalty, Reviews, Team, Business Hours, Policies, Integrations, Billing and Settings. Staff members see a shorter menu focused on their own bookings, calendar, availability and profile.
<!-- src: SP-176, SP-077, DOC-238 -->

## What can I change in Business Settings?
Business Settings in the Accez service provider portal has three tabs: Profile, Business Details and Change Password. Business Details require a service radius of at least 1 km, a business location, a default currency and a monthly ad budget.
<!-- src: SP-177, SP-178 -->

## What can I control on the Policies page?
The Policies page in the Accez service provider portal is where you set your business timezone, your upfront deposit percentage, and whether customer reminders are sent. Each of these affects what your customers see and how they book on the Accez marketplace, so it is worth checking them when you first set up.
<!-- src: SP-086, SP-123, SP-159 -->
