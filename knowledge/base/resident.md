## How do I get access to the Accez resident portal?

Your property manager can create an account for you. You then activate it from a link, set a password, verify your phone with a 4-digit code sent by SMS, and sign in to the Accez resident portal. If you book a stay through your property manager's Accez booking page, an account can also be created for you from your booking details. If the sign-in page does not recognise you, contact your property manager.
<!-- src: RES-005, RES-009, BKG-023, DOC-192 -->

## How do I sign in to the Accez resident portal?

You sign in with the email address and password on your account. If two-factor sign-in is switched on for your account, you are also asked for a 4-digit code after your password. After signing in, you are taken to your property's home page. If you manage more than one property, you are asked to choose which one to open.
<!-- src: RES-001, RES-002, RES-013 -->

## What if I am asked for a code when I sign in?

If two-factor sign-in is on for your account, the Accez resident portal asks for a 4-digit code sent by SMS or email. You can ask for a new code on that screen. If both SMS and email are available, you can switch between them. You can manage two-factor sign-in from the tabs on your profile page.
<!-- src: RES-002, RES-003, RES-082 -->

## What happens if I type the wrong password?

If you enter the wrong password three times when signing in to the Accez resident portal, a password-reset email is sent to you automatically. Use the link in that email to choose a new password. Once you are signed in, you can also change your password from the password tab on your profile page.
<!-- src: RES-006, DOC-175, RES-082 -->

## What does "Property Assignment Pending" mean?

If you sign in to the Accez resident portal and see "Property Assignment Pending", your login is not yet linked to a property. Your property manager has to assign you to a property before you can use the portal. Please contact your property manager and ask them to assign you.
<!-- src: RES-012 -->

## What can I find in the Accez resident portal menu?

On a computer the menu lists Home, Book a Place, Services, My Bookings, Guest Resources, Work Orders, Marketplace, Property Feeds, Chat Messages and About Property. On a phone the bottom bar shows Home, Services, Bookings and Menu. Your account menu has My Profile, My Work Orders, Payment History and Sign Out.
<!-- src: RES-014, RES-081 -->

## Why are some sections greyed out?

In the Accez resident portal, sections such as Services, Guest Resources, Marketplace and Property Feeds appear greyed out with "Available during your stay" until you have an active reservation. Book a Place and My Bookings stay available even without an active stay, so you can look at past bookings or book again.
<!-- src: RES-015, RES-016 -->

## What does the home page show when I have no active stay?

If you have no active stay, the Accez resident portal home page shows "No active stay" together with a "Book again" button. Use it to start a new booking at your property. Book a Place and My Bookings stay available even when you have no active stay, while some other sections are greyed out until you have an active reservation.
<!-- src: RES-018, RES-016 -->

## How do I book a place to stay without an account?

You do not need an account to browse or book on the Accez booking page. You get to it through a direct link from your property manager, which is specific to their company. At checkout you enter your name, email and phone number. If the link is wrong, the page says the company or booking page was not found, so ask your property manager for the correct link.
<!-- src: BKG-001, BKG-002, BKG-003, DOC-192 -->

## How do I search for a place on the Accez booking page?

You can search by check-in and check-out dates, city and area. You can also filter by name or reference, accommodation type, bathrooms, bedrooms, price range and features, and sort by reference or price. Results show 12 per page. Only units your property manager has listed for short-term rental appear, and units already booked for your dates are left out.
<!-- src: BKG-004, BKG-005, BKG-006, BKG-008, DOC-195 -->

## What are the steps to book on the Accez booking page?

The steps are Dates, then Add-ons (only if the unit has add-ons), then Checkout, then a confirmation screen. If you reload the page, your dates, guests and contact details are kept so you can pick up where you stopped. Identical rooms of the same type are shown as one card with a count of how many are available.
<!-- src: BKG-010, BKG-033, BKG-007 -->

## How do I book a place in the Accez resident portal?

Open Book a Place in the Accez resident portal. Choose your check-in date, check-out date and number of guests to see the rental units at your property. Open a unit to see its details and pick your dates, then continue to checkout.
<!-- src: RES-025, RES-027 -->

## What information is on a unit page?

A unit page in the Accez resident portal shows photos, amenities, house rules, and the check-in and check-out times. It also has a "Contact Host" button that starts a chat with your property manager if you have a question before booking.
<!-- src: RES-026, RES-072 -->

## How does the date calendar work?

The calendar marks days that are already reserved. If the range you pick includes a day that is not available, the Accez resident portal does not accept it, so choose different dates. On the Accez booking page, past days and fully booked days are disabled. For room types with several identical rooms, a day is blocked only when every room of that type is taken.
<!-- src: RES-027, BKG-009, DOC-202 -->

## Is there a limit on the number of guests?

Yes. The number of guests cannot be more than the maximum guests set for the unit you are booking. If you need to bring more people than a unit allows, look for a larger unit or ask your property manager.
<!-- src: RES-030, BKG-011 -->

## Do I need to sign in to reserve in the resident portal?

If you are not signed in when you press Reserve in the Accez resident portal, you are asked to log in and then returned to the same reservation page. The same happens if your session expires during checkout. Before confirming, you must tick that you agree to the terms of service and the check-in and check-out policy. Payment takes place on the next page.
<!-- src: DOC-140, DOC-147, RES-031, RES-028 -->

## What if I do not have an account when I book from the resident portal?

The sign-up page in the Accez resident portal only works when you arrive from a booking you have started, with the property, unit and dates already chosen. If you open it without a booking, it asks you to start by browsing. After you sign up with a booking in progress, you are taken straight to that reservation's payment page.
<!-- src: RES-010, RES-011, DOC-174 -->

## Can I add extras to my booking?

Yes, where your property offers them. At checkout in the Accez resident portal, optional extras appear under "Enhance Your Stay", grouped by category. On the Accez booking page, add-ons are grouped by category, with quantity buttons, and their price is added to your total. Some add-ons need approval, in which case your property manager approves or rejects them and emails you the result.
<!-- src: RES-032, BKG-015, DOC-148 -->

## How do I pay for my booking?

You pay by card at checkout. Accez uses Stripe and Tap Payments. If your property manager has not finished setting up payments, paying shows an error, and you should contact them. Your card details are entered on the payment provider's form.
<!-- src: RES-033, BKG-017, BKG-019, D-10 -->

## Can I pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay?

On some payment screens, buttons for Apple Pay, Google Pay or Link appear, but only when your device and browser support them. Which wallet buttons appear depends on your device and browser. If you do not see them, you can still pay by card at checkout in the Accez resident portal or on the Accez booking page.
<!-- src: DOC-137, RES-034 -->

## What happens if I pay with Tap Payments?

If your booking is paid through Tap Payments, you may be sent to a secure card verification page and then back to the Accez booking page. The booking is confirmed when Tap Payments confirms your payment, not just when you return to the page.
<!-- src: BKG-018 -->

## What if my payment does not go through?

If your card is declined, you see a message asking you to try a different payment method, and no reservation is created. On the Accez booking page you are returned to the confirm step with an error, and your details are kept so you can try again. If the problem continues, contact your property manager.
<!-- src: DOC-145, DOC-197, BKG-033 -->

## What status does my booking have, and how do I pay for a pending one?

A booking is first saved as Pending. Once your payment is verified, it becomes Confirmed and Paid. If a reservation is still unpaid, My Bookings in the Accez resident portal shows a "Pay Now" button that opens the payment page, and your property home page can show a banner reminding you about unpaid reservations.
<!-- src: BKG-020, DOC-139, RES-023, DOC-149, DOC-203 -->

## What happens after I pay on the Accez booking page?

You see a "Booking confirmed" screen. If your email already has an account, the booking is added to your existing Accez resident portal account. Otherwise an account is created for you. New guests may be asked to set a password on this screen and can skip. You are also sent a booking-confirmation email with a check-in link, and the booking appears in your Payment History.
<!-- src: BKG-022, BKG-023, BKG-024, BKG-025, BKG-027 -->

## Which emails will I receive about my booking?

Guests receive emails for a new reservation, payment confirmation, add-on decisions, a check-in reminder before arrival and a check-out summary. The booking confirmation email contains a check-in link. The check-in reminder is sent once, and is not sent if the reservation has been cancelled.
<!-- src: DOC-171, DOC-170, BKG-025 -->

## What does My Bookings show?

My Bookings in the Accez resident portal lists both your stays and your service bookings, with counts, a search box, and filters for type, status and date. The status filter offers Confirmed, Pending, Cancelled, Check-in and Check-out. Each booking opens a details page.
<!-- src: RES-021, RES-022 -->

## What is on the booking details page?

The booking details page shows your dates, guests, room type, a payment breakdown including the cleaning fee and add-ons, and any special requests. It also has a directions link, a "Contact Property" button and a Print button.
<!-- src: RES-024 -->

## How do I check in online?

You check in with an online form, reached from a link in your booking emails. It asks for personal details, your expected arrival time, an optional address, an emergency contact, any additional guests, and ID or passport photos. The form needs you to accept the required terms. If a reservation cannot be checked in, you see a message, and if you have already checked in, you see a notice saying so.
<!-- src: RES-053, RES-055, RES-056, DOC-170, BKG-025 -->

## What files can I upload for check-in?

ID or passport photos for online check-in, which you add on the check-in form reached from the link in your booking emails, must be JPG, JPEG or PNG files, no larger than 5 MB each. Other file types are not accepted for these photos.
<!-- src: RES-054 -->

## What if my property manager asks me to correct my check-in details?

Your property manager can ask you to correct the details you submitted. When they do, you reopen your check-in link, which opens in correction mode, update the information and submit it again. This is the same online check-in form you used the first time.
<!-- src: RES-058 -->

## How do check-in and check-out times work?

The unit page in the Accez resident portal shows the check-in and check-out times for that unit. Online check-in is a form you fill in before you arrive. I do not have detailed check-out steps, so for questions about leaving your stay, contact your property manager through Chat Messages.
<!-- src: RES-026, RES-053, RES-071 -->

## How do I see my payments and invoices?

Payment History in the Accez resident portal lists your invoices with their status (Paid, Pending, Failed or Refunded), your totals and a spending chart. You can export the list as a CSV file. Bookings made on the Accez booking page also appear here once the invoice is created.
<!-- src: RES-036, DOC-164, BKG-027 -->

## Can I cancel my booking or get a refund?

Whether a refund is possible, and how much it is, depends on your booking. I cannot give you amounts or timing. Please contact your property manager directly, using the "Contact Property" button on your booking details page or Chat Messages in the Accez resident portal, and they will tell you what applies.
<!-- src: D-08, RES-024, RES-071 -->

## How do I book a service such as cleaning?

Open Services in the Accez resident portal. It lists services with their price or "Free", their availability, and how far ahead you need to book. You need an active reservation to browse. When booking, you pick date(s), time slots and, where offered, a sub-category and the number of people or quantity. Fully booked slots are shown as full.
<!-- src: RES-061, RES-062, DOC-150, DOC-152, DOC-153 -->

## How do I pay for a service?

Paid services are paid by card when you book them. Free services are reserved without any payment. Some services have a minimum lead time shown as "Book X days ahead", and if you book too close to the start, you are asked to choose a later date.
<!-- src: RES-063, DOC-152 -->

## Can I buy extras for my stay?

The Services area of the Accez resident portal lets you buy extras for your stay, and the charge is made instantly. The Extras tab is not available unless you have an active reservation to attach them to.
<!-- src: RES-064, DOC-154 -->

## Can I book services from the Accez marketplace?

The Accez marketplace lists independent businesses that provide services. Customers can browse by category, search by name and find businesses on a map. They see each business's services with durations and prices, book within the business's opening hours, and can leave a rating and review afterwards. Services booked through the resident portal are delivered by these providers.
<!-- src: WEB-065, WEB-062 -->

## How do I report a maintenance problem?

Use Work Orders in the Accez resident portal to submit a request. You enter a title, category, problem type, unit, location, preferred time window, whether staff may enter, and a description. You can flag if you have a pet, or if you need a quote first. At least one photo is required. You see only your own requests, in tabs for All, Pending, Ongoing and Completed, and can search them.
<!-- src: RES-066, RES-067, RES-068, DOC-184 -->

## How do I message my property manager?

Use Chat Messages in the Accez resident portal to chat with your property manager. You can send attachments, see unread marks and see when they are online. The "Contact Host" button on a unit page also starts a chat. Messages you send while offline are sent when your connection returns. How quickly the manager replies depends on them.
<!-- src: RES-071, RES-072, DOC-176, DOC-178 -->

## Can I get automatic replies in chat?

Some properties switch on an AI auto-reply option. If yours has, common questions may be answered within seconds, and the reply is marked with an "Auto-reply" badge. It is optional for each property, so you may not see it.
<!-- src: DOC-177 -->

## How do I view my rental lease or sale contract?

If you have a rental lease or a sale contract on file, a "My Contracts" menu item appears in the Accez resident portal. A rental lease shows rent, term, handover, the installment schedule with paid, pending or late status, escalations, security deposits, extra fees and clauses. For a sale contract you can open the contract document in a new tab. These pages are for viewing. Ask your property manager how to pay rent.
<!-- src: RES-017, RES-038, RES-039 -->

## What can I do on my profile page?

Your profile in the Accez resident portal has tabs for your account details, changing your password, and two-factor sign-in, which can use SMS or email. It is also where you find Sign Out, next to My Work Orders and Payment History, in the account menu.
<!-- src: RES-082, RES-081 -->

## Is the Accez resident portal available in Arabic?

Yes. The Accez resident portal and the Accez booking page can be switched between English and Arabic, with a right-to-left layout for Arabic. On the booking page, your property manager can also customise how the page looks, including the About and Help pages.
<!-- src: DOC-187, BKG-034 -->

## How do I contact the property company from the booking page?

The header and footer of the Accez booking page show the company's phone number and email address as links, if the company has filled them in. Inside the Accez resident portal, you can chat with your property manager or use the Contact Property button on a booking.
<!-- src: BKG-035, RES-071, RES-024 -->

## What is the community feed?

Property Feeds in the Accez resident portal is a community feed where your property manager and residents post. It supports announcements, events, questions and polls, with reactions and comments. Residents are joined to the property community when their lease is activated. Like other stay-only sections, it can appear greyed out until you have an active reservation.
<!-- src: DOC-182, DOC-183, RES-015 -->

## Can I sell or rent out items in the marketplace?

The Accez resident portal has a Marketplace where residents can list items for sale or rent in their community. Listing is limited to residents who have a unit, so guests cannot create listings. A listing reported by other residents may be hidden until the property manager reviews it.
<!-- src: DOC-179, DOC-180, DOC-181 -->

## Can I leave a review after my stay?

Yes. After you check out, you can leave a star review for your stay. Only the overall star rating is required. You can also add optional sub-ratings, a title and a written review, and you can edit your review until its edit window ends. The Accez resident portal does not state how long that window is.
<!-- src: DOC-173 -->
