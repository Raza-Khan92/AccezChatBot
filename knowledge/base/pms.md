## How do I sign up for the Accez property management system?
You sign up on the sign-up page of the Accez property management system. You enter your first name, last name, email, phone number (with a country-code picker, Saudi Arabia +966 by default), a password and the password again. You also tick the SMS consent and Terms boxes and complete a reCAPTCHA. There is no separate "start free trial" page: you choose your plan later, during onboarding. A phone number that is already registered is rejected.
<!-- src: DOC-001, PMS-001, PMS-002, DOC-006 -->

## Do I have to verify my email and phone after signing up?
Yes. In the Accez property management system, a new account cannot log in until both the email address and the phone number are verified. You enter a 6-digit code sent by email and a 4-digit code sent by SMS. Each code has a 6-minute countdown, and you can ask for a new code once the timer reaches zero. When both are verified, a new property manager is taken to the company setup wizard.
<!-- src: DOC-007, DOC-008, PMS-005, PMS-007, DOC-009 -->

## What does the setup wizard ask me?
After verification, the Accez property management system walks a new property manager through a chat-style setup wizard with four phases: Choose Plan, Company Details, Set Up Property and Configure Unit. The company step asks for your company name, business phone, an optional website and your address. In the unit step you choose long-term or short-term: short-term asks for a unit title, nightly price and check-in and check-out times, and long-term asks for a rental price.
<!-- src: PMS-022, PMS-023, DOC-024, DOC-026 -->

## Does the setup wizard create all of my units?
No. In the Accez property management system, the wizard question about how many units your property has does not create that many units. Only the one unit you configure in the wizard is created. You add the rest afterwards on the Units screen, one by one, as identical rooms in one go, or by importing a spreadsheet.
<!-- src: DOC-019, PMS-149, PMS-168 -->

## Can I leave the setup wizard and come back?
Yes. If you refresh the setup wizard in the Accez property management system, or come back from the Stripe payment page, it resumes where you left off. This uses saved browser session data, so it works in the same browser session. If you skip or have not finished company setup, the dashboard shows a Set Up Your Company screen with Get Started and Skip for now. Skipping lasts only for the current browser session.
<!-- src: DOC-027, PMS-025 -->

## What company details does the Set Up Your Company screen ask for?
The Set Up Your Company screen in the Accez property management system asks for your company name and phone, then an optional website and a 10-digit National ID, and then your address: country, city, street and ZIP are required, while district and address line 2 are optional. You must complete your company profile before you can add a property. If it is not complete, a company setup window opens first.
<!-- src: PMS-025, PMS-117, DOC-034 -->

## How do I log in?
In the Accez property management system you can log in with your email and password, or with the Sign in with Google button. After login, property managers and unit owners land on the main dashboard, and staff members land on the staff dashboard. If two-factor authentication is switched on for your account, you are asked for a 4-digit code sent by SMS or email, and you can ask for a resend or switch to the other method.
<!-- src: PMS-008, PMS-009, PMS-010 -->

## What user types does the Accez property management system have?
The Accez property management system serves three kinds of users: the property manager (the company account), unit owners and staff. Staff and unit owners do not sign up themselves; the property manager invites them. Service providers use their own separate app, and a service provider who logs in here is redirected there.
<!-- src: PMS-020, PMS-509, PMS-004 -->

## How do staff members and unit owners get access?
In the Accez property management system, staff and unit owners are invited by the property manager. The person receives an email with a link, sets a strong password (8 or more characters with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character), and then continues. An invited staff member lands on the staff dashboard. An invited unit owner is sent to the login page.
<!-- src: PMS-018, PMS-019 -->

## How do I reset or change my password?
In the Accez property management system, you can request a password-reset email from the forgot-password page by entering your email. The reset link is valid for 15 minutes. The new password must be at least 8 characters and include an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character. When you are logged in, you can also change your password under Settings, Change Password, by entering your current password, a new one and a confirmation.

## How do I turn on two-factor authentication?
In the Accez property management system, go to Settings, Security. There you can turn two-factor authentication on or off and choose to receive codes by SMS, email or both. A method can only be chosen if that phone number or email is already verified. You must enter your password to turn it off. You can also change the phone number used for two-factor authentication and verify it with an SMS code.

## What are the limits of the Free plan?
On the Free plan of the Accez property management system you can have 1 property, 5 units, 5 residents and 2 staff members. Each month you can have up to 10 check-ins and 10 work orders. When you try to go beyond a limit, the system stops you and asks you to upgrade.

In the Accez property management system, adding more than the Free plan allows is rejected with a message to upgrade. Once a property is at its unit limit, further unit additions are blocked too. The same applies to residents, monthly check-ins and monthly work orders.

## How do I add a resident?
In the Accez property management system, a manager adds a resident to a property by entering the unit, first name, last name, email and phone, plus optional gender, date of birth and national ID. This creates a resident login account and emails an invitation link to verify the account. The manager can resend the invitation from the resident list for unverified residents. Staff can add residents if their permission allows.
<!-- src: PMS-326, PMS-327, PMS-336, DOC-112 -->

## Can I block or remove a resident?
Yes. In the Accez property management system, a manager can block or unblock a resident, and the resident is notified that their property access is suspended or restored. Deleting a resident in the list removes them from the current property only; their login account is kept. You are warned if the resident is in several properties.
<!-- src: PMS-330, PMS-331, DOC-112 -->

## Can I edit residents or see their status?
In the Accez property management system, a manager can edit a resident, including changing or removing units, and a resident can be linked to several units. The resident list shows each resident as Blocked, Verified or Unverified, and has a search.
<!-- src: PMS-332, PMS-333 -->

## Is there a limit on residents?
On the Free plan of the Accez property management system, you can have up to 5 residents in total. If you try to add more, the system stops you and asks you to upgrade. For the resident limits on paid plans, please check the plan details in your account or contact the Accez team.
<!-- src: D-05 -->

## How does the Inbox work?
In the Accez property management system, a property manager can chat in real time with residents from the Inbox, with online and offline indicators and a notification sound. You start a new chat by picking from your residents list, and can send text messages with attachments. The sidebar Inbox shows an unread badge, and messages are marked read when you open a chat. The Inbox is in-app only, and unit owners do not get it.
<!-- src: PMS-475, PMS-476, PMS-478, PMS-479 -->

## Can I delete a chat?
Yes. In the Accez property management system, a property manager can delete a chat from the Inbox. Deleting a chat deletes all of the messages in it, so make sure you no longer need the conversation before you confirm.
<!-- src: PMS-477 -->

## How do email templates work?
In the Accez property management system, a property manager manages email templates under Communication, Email Templates. You can list, search, create, edit, delete and preview templates and see whether each is active. You can have one template per type. Templates are edited as text fields and a subject, not raw HTML. Default templates are created the first time the list is empty.
<!-- src: PMS-081, PMS-484 -->

## Which emails can I customise?
In the Accez property management system, customised templates are used for reservation confirmation, check-in, cancellation, payment request and check-in reminder emails, service reservation confirmation and cancellation emails, service payment emails and ticket updates. Otherwise the built-in default is sent. A custom template is used only when it is active. You can use variables such as resident name, property name, check-in and check-out dates, total price, access code, WiFi details and house rules.
<!-- src: PMS-486, PMS-082, PMS-487 -->

## Can I post to a community feed?
Yes. In the Accez property management system, a property manager can publish posts, events and polls for a property, with titles, descriptions and images. An event needs a location and date and time, and a poll needs a question and at least two options. You can edit and delete feed items, including a resident's post in your own property, and filter by type, search by title and sort by newest, oldest, most liked or least liked.
<!-- src: PMS-510, PMS-511, PMS-512 -->

## Who can react to the feed?
In the Accez property management system, residents, staff and unit owners can like, comment and vote in polls on the community feed, and the property manager can see who voted. A property can switch the Feed, Events, Polls and Marketplace features on or off in its settings, which hides the matching tabs.
<!-- src: PMS-513, PMS-515 -->

## What is the community marketplace in a property?
In the Accez property management system, a property's Marketplace tab is a community listing board. A property manager can create a listing with title, description, category, condition (new or used), price, status (available, sold or inactive), an optional unit and images. You can edit, delete and mark a listing as sold, and view interested users.
<!-- src: PMS-516, PMS-517 -->

## Can I pin local places for residents?
Yes. Under Directions in the Accez property management system, a property manager can pin locations for residents with a name, description, category, map pin and an optional image, and edit or remove them. Name and map location are required, and there are many categories such as restaurant, cafe, park, gym, supermarket, pharmacy, hospital, school, bank and airport.
<!-- src: PMS-520 -->

## What is the Guests tab in a property?
In the Accez property management system, the Guests tab is a Guest Resources library. A property manager creates information items with a title, an emoji icon and rich-text content for guests, and can edit, delete, feature and search them. It does not list guests or reservations.
<!-- src: PMS-271, PMS-521 -->

## What services can I offer to residents?
In the Accez property management system, a property manager creates a company-wide service with a name, description and image, then attaches it to individual properties. At a property a service can be free or paid. Paid services use quantity, hourly or daily pricing with a rate. Hourly and quantity services need at least one weekly availability slot with a maximum number of reservations per day. A service can be switched between available and unavailable.
<!-- src: PMS-491, PMS-494, PMS-496, PMS-500, DOC-088 -->

## Can I set up sub-categories, a default staff member or a lead time for a service?
Yes. In the Accez property management system, a property manager can add optional sub-categories to a property service, assign a default staff member who fulfils every reservation of that service, and set a minimum booking lead time in hours from the edit form. Deleting a service also deletes the property services using it and all of its service reservations.
<!-- src: PMS-497, PMS-498, PMS-493 -->

## How do I book a service for a resident?
In the Accez property management system, a property manager can book a service for a resident by choosing the service, an optional sub-category, the resident, single-day or multi-day dates and a time slot or daily access. A paid service booked by a manager is created as pending payment, and the resident pays through the resident portal. Free services need no payment.
<!-- src: PMS-274, PMS-275 -->

## How do I follow and assign service reservations?
In the Accez property management system, service reservations appear in a table you can filter by All, Confirmed, Pending Payment or Canceled and search. A property manager can re-send the payment email for unpaid ones, and can assign a service reservation to a staff member and reassign it. A warning appears if that staff member is busy.
<!-- src: PMS-276, PMS-277 -->

## Do service providers use the same app?
No. In the Accez property management system, service providers have their own separate app. The property manager portal has no provider menu, and a service provider who logs in here is redirected to the provider domain. Property managers, unit owners and staff use the property management portal.
<!-- src: PMS-509, PMS-020 -->

## What does the property manager dashboard show?
The dashboard of the Accez property management system gives a property manager an overview with cards for properties, tenants, monthly check-ins and services, plus work order status (pending, in progress, completed), recent reservations, check-in analytics, property performance, staff analytics and quick actions. On the Free plan the dashboard also shows how much of your plan limits you have used.
<!-- src: PMS-066, PMS-067, DOC-029 -->

## What reports and analytics are available?
The Accez property management system shows analytics in several places. Billing has a revenue overview with a 6-month trend and a transactions list you can export to CSV. Real Estate has a Portfolio dashboard and project performance panels with Excel export. Unit owners get earnings analytics and statements. The dashboard also includes staff analytics.
<!-- src: PMS-061, DOC-091, PMS-442, PMS-394, PMS-395, PMS-064, PMS-341, PMS-474 -->

## Can I connect Google Analytics or Meta Pixel?
In the Accez property management system, the Integrations page lets a property manager connect Google Tag Manager (container ID), Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID) and Meta Pixel (pixel ID) by pasting an ID or full snippet, and disconnect them. The IDs are meant for the company's public booking pages.
