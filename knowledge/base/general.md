## What is Accez

Accez Cloud is a platform that offers property management solutions and a marketplace for service providers, one platform connecting both. On the property management side there are sales properties and rental properties, units, contracts and the sales path, and many other tools. On the service provider side you run your service business from one place and customers book appointments. Accez is built for the Saudi Arabia market and works in English and Arabic.
<!-- src: owner -->

## Who is Accez for

Accez serves two groups of businesses. On the property side it is for property managers, real estate companies, owners, vacation rentals, hotels and serviced apartments, and developers and agencies that sell units. On the marketplace side it is for service providers, meaning any independent service business. Residents and guests use Accez too, to book services and amenities. Accez is built for Saudi Arabia and works in English and Arabic.

## How the property side and the service provider side connect

Accez is one platform with two sides that connect. Property managers run their properties, units and operations. Service providers run their service businesses and take customer bookings. Residents and tenants can book amenities and services directly through the platform, and those services are delivered by providers on the Accez marketplace, where any independent business can join. A service provider account and a property manager account are separate account types.

## Are property manager and service provider accounts separate

Yes. On Accez, a service provider is its own account type, completely separate from a property manager account. Property managers sign up and sign in through the property manager portal. Service providers sign up and sign in through the provider portal. Service provider pricing is also separate from Accez property management plans. If someone runs both kinds of business and wants to know how to set that up, the Accez team can advise through the website contact form.

## The property management side

On the property management side Accez covers reservations, work orders, owners and payouts, and sales and contracts, with rental and sale properties each having their own units. The property manager portal holds property, unit and room records, leases and tenancies, reservations and stays, work orders and maintenance, staff with schedules, resident and guest communication, a customer record, owner earnings statements and payout records, and billing for your own subscription. Which features you get depends on your plan.
<!-- src: owner, WEB-001, WEB-041, WEB-035 -->

## The service provider side

On the service provider side you run your service business from one place and customers book appointments. In the provider portal you set up your business profile and venue, publish services with their durations and prices, manage your calendar and availability, add staff with their schedules and assign bookings to them, and see your bookings and billing. Accez lists you and takes the booking. Accez does not perform the service or set your prices.

## Is a service provider only a salon or barber

No. A service provider is not only a salon or barber. It can be a pet clinic or any other service business. Accez says any independent business can list, not only property-related trades. Examples named on the Accez website include salons and barbers, spas and wellness studios, fitness coaches, photographers and home services. If you are unsure whether your kind of business fits, you can ask the Accez team through the website contact form.

## How customers find and book service providers

Customers can browse the Accez marketplace by category, search by name and find businesses on a map. They see each business's service list with durations and prices, and they can book inside the business's opening hours. After a booking, a customer can leave a rating and review. A provider's public listing shows photos, an about section, location, opening hours for every day of the week and the full service list.

## What Accez does and does not do

Accez is software and a marketplace. For property managers it is operations software for your business, so you operate your properties with it and Accez does not operate them with you. Accez does not own, lease, manage or maintain properties and is not a broker or letting agent. For service providers, Accez lists the business and takes the booking but does not perform the service, set prices or employ the provider or staff.
